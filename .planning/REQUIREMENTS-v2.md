# Requirements: Metropolis v2.0 - Las Vegas Expansion

**Defined:** 2026-01-25
**Core Value:** The city feels alive and worth watching — Vegas adds dramatic visual spectacle

## v2 Requirements

Requirements for Las Vegas expansion. Each maps to roadmap phases.

### Infrastructure

- [x] **INFRA-01**: Route 66 highway extends from Metropolis to Vegas with highway marker sign
- [x] **INFRA-02**: The Strip main road with sidewalks appropriate for Vegas layout
- [x] **INFRA-03**: Vegas zone has independent sunny weather (ignores Metropolis weather cycle)
- [x] **INFRA-04**: Temperature billboard displays Vegas temperature (always hot, 95-115°F)

### Landmarks

- [x] **LAND-01**: Luxor Pyramid with black glass exterior and sky beam at night
- [x] **LAND-02**: Bellagio with elegant facade and fountain lake in front
- [x] **LAND-03**: Paris Eiffel Tower replica at recognizable scale
- [x] **LAND-04**: Caesars Palace with Roman columns and grand entrance
- [x] **LAND-05**: Excalibur Castle with medieval turrets and colorful towers

### Lighting

- [ ] **LITE-01**: All casino buildings have extensive exterior lights (off during day)
- [ ] **LITE-02**: Casino lights activate at night with Vegas-style brightness
- [ ] **LITE-03**: Luxor sky beam visible at night shooting into sky
- [ ] **LITE-04**: Landmark-specific accent lighting (different colors per building)

### Entertainment

- [ ] **ENTR-01**: Bellagio fountain show triggers periodically (every 2-3 minutes)
- [ ] **ENTR-02**: Fountain show draws tourist crowds to watch
- [ ] **ENTR-03**: Wedding chapels with couples exiting periodically
- [ ] **ENTR-04**: Elvis impersonators walking The Strip

### Pedestrians

- [ ] **PED-01**: Tourist pedestrians distinct from regular pedestrians
- [ ] **PED-02**: Tourists walk slower than regular pedestrians
- [ ] **PED-03**: Tourists stop to take photos of landmarks
- [ ] **PED-04**: Tourists gather to watch fountain shows

### Vehicles

- [ ] **VEH-01**: Exotic cars (Lamborghinis, Ferraris) with distinctive shapes
- [ ] **VEH-02**: Exotic cars drive on The Strip with flashy colors
- [ ] **VEH-03**: Open-top tourist buses for sightseeing
- [ ] **VEH-04**: Tourist buses follow Strip route

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Casino interiors | Exterior observation only, complexity |
| Gambling mechanics | Visual simulation, not game mechanics |
| Real-time show scheduling | Simplified periodic triggers |
| Venetian gondolas | Scope control, 5 landmarks chosen |
| Stratosphere tower | Scope control, 5 landmarks chosen |
| Sound/audio | Consistent with v1 visual focus |
| Water effects in fountains | Particle complexity, simplified visual |
